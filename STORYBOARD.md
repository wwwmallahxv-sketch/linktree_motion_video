## Notes
- The scene 4 page is modeled on the real Chiken Hut page. On the live page, WhatsApp is a round icon next to Facebook and Instagram, not a full-width button, so that icon is the one that lights up. The live page has no map embed, only the address card with «فتح في الخرائط». The map pin and route animation grow out of that card.
- The students' group page shows a real person's photo and contact details. In scene 7 they appear small and slightly blurred, so the page reads as a layout and not as a person's contact info.
- Until the voiceover arrives, the timings above are estimates. Everything gets re-timed to `assets/voiceover.mp3` once it's added.
